Features

Everything you need to unlock your documents

A complete production-grade RAG platform with AI-powered learning tools built in.

PDF Ingestion

Upload any PDF. It's automatically chunked, embedded with Gemini's 3072-dim model, and stored in Qdrant — ready to query in seconds.

AI Chat (RAG)

Ask natural language questions. Get grounded, cited answers generated by Gemini 2.5 Flash — sourced directly from your documents.

Quiz Generation

Generate multiple-choice quizzes from any knowledge base with one click. Each question comes with an AI explanation of the correct answer.

Flashcard Decks

AI-generated front/back study cards with smooth flip animations. Study any concept from your knowledge bases with spaced repetition in mind.

Knowledge Bases

Organise your documents into isolated knowledge bases. Each KB gets its own vector collection — no cross-contamination of context.

Async Processing

PDF ingestion runs in the background via Inngest — throttled, rate-limited, and fully durable. Upload and move on; the pipeline handles the rest.

How It Works

From upload to insight in four steps

A battle-tested RAG pipeline under the hood — simple on the surface.

01

Upload your PDFs

Create a Knowledge Base, then drag-and-drop your documents. Files are securely stored and ingestion kicks off immediately in the background.

02

Automatic chunking & embedding

LlamaIndex splits your text into 1000-token chunks with 200-token overlap. Each chunk is embedded into a 3072-dimensional vector via Gemini Embedding.

03

Stored in Qdrant

Vectors are upserted into a dedicated Qdrant collection per knowledge base. Cosine similarity search retrieves the top-k most relevant chunks at query time.

04

Gemini answers your question

Retrieved chunks are assembled into a grounded prompt and sent to Gemini 2.5 Flash, which returns a structured Markdown answer with cited sources.

About

Built for production, not just demos

RAGbase is a full-stack, self-hosted knowledge platform — open source and designed to run anywhere with Docker.

🧠

State-of-the-art AI

Gemini 2.5 Flash for generation and gemini-embedding-2 (3072-dim) for embeddings — the same models powering Google's flagship products.

⚡

Production architecture

FastAPI + Uvicorn backend, Qdrant vector DB, Inngest durable job queue, JWT auth, SQLite persistence — every component is production-grade.

🐳

One-command deploy

docker compose up --build starts the entire stack — backend, frontend, vector DB, and workflow engine — with zero configuration.

🔐

Secure & private

JWT-based authentication, bcrypt password hashing, per-user knowledge base isolation. Your data stays on your infrastructure.
